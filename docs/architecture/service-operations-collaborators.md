# Can't Run Alone - Service / Operations / Collaborators

Target architecture, as submitted for Deliverable 2. Phase 1 builds a subset of it; see [Phase 1 scope](README.md#phase-1-scope).

| Service | Operations | Collaborators |
|---|---|---|
| **Buddy Discovery Service**<br>Core subdomain<br>*Data: Discovery DB (PostgreSQL + PostGIS)* | 1. `searchRunners()`<br>2. `sendBuddyRequest()`<br>3. `getBuddyRequests()`<br>4. `respondToBuddyRequest()`<br>5. `listBuddies()`<br>6. `blockRunner()`<br>7. `expireBuddyRequests()`<br>&emsp;- scheduled<br>8. `updateCandidate()`<br>&emsp;- on `ProfileUpdated`, `RunnerVerified`, `VerificationRejected`, `AccountSuspended`, `RatingUpdated` | 1. **Event Broker (Kafka)**<br>&emsp;> publish `BuddyRequestSent` (Notification)<br>&emsp;> publish `BuddyRequestAnswered` (Notification)<br>&emsp;> consume `ProfileUpdated` (Profile)<br>&emsp;> consume `RunnerVerified` (Trust & Safety)<br>&emsp;> consume `VerificationRejected` (Trust & Safety)<br>&emsp;> consume `AccountSuspended` (Trust & Safety)<br>&emsp;> consume `RatingUpdated` (Reputation) |
| **Run Conditions Service**<br>Core subdomain<br>*Data: Weather cache (Redis, NoSQL)* | 1. `getRunConditions()`<br>&emsp;- gRPC<br>2. `suggestAlternativeSlots()`<br>&emsp;- gRPC | 1. **Weather & Air-Quality Provider** (Weather Adapter)<br>&emsp;> `getForecast()`<br>&emsp;> `getAirQuality()` |
| **Run Scheduling Service**<br>Supporting subdomain<br>*Data: Run DB (PostgreSQL + PostGIS)* | 1. `previewRunConditions()`<br>2. `createRun()`<br>3. `updateRun()`<br>4. `cancelRun()`<br>5. `browseUpcomingRuns()`<br>6. `joinRun()`<br>7. `leaveRun()`<br>8. `sendRunReminders()`<br>&emsp;- scheduled<br>9. `recheckForecasts()`<br>&emsp;- scheduled<br>10. `endRun()`<br>&emsp;- scheduled<br>11. `updateRunnerStatus()`<br>&emsp;- on `RunnerVerified`, `VerificationRejected`, `AccountSuspended` | 1. **Run Conditions Service** (gRPC)<br>&emsp;> `getRunConditions()`<br>&emsp;> `suggestAlternativeSlots()`<br>2. **Event Broker (Kafka)**<br>&emsp;> publish `RunScheduled` (Notification)<br>&emsp;> publish `RunChanged` (Notification)<br>&emsp;> publish `RunCancelled` (Notification, Reputation)<br>&emsp;> publish `ReminderDue` (Notification)<br>&emsp;> publish `ForecastChanged` (Notification)<br>&emsp;> publish `RunEnded` (Reputation)<br>&emsp;> consume `RunnerVerified` (Trust & Safety)<br>&emsp;> consume `VerificationRejected` (Trust & Safety)<br>&emsp;> consume `AccountSuspended` (Trust & Safety) |
| **Profile Service**<br>Supporting subdomain<br>*Data: Profile DB (PostgreSQL)* | 1. `createProfile()`<br>2. `updateProfile()`<br>3. `setFieldVisibility()`<br>4. `getPublicProfile()` | 1. **Event Broker (Kafka)**<br>&emsp;> publish `ProfileUpdated` (Buddy Discovery) |
| **Reputation Service**<br>Supporting subdomain<br>*Data: Reputation DB (PostgreSQL)* | 1. `openAttendance()`<br>&emsp;- on `RunEnded`<br>2. `confirmAttendance()`<br>3. `closeAttendanceWindows()`<br>&emsp;- scheduled<br>4. `rateParticipant()`<br>5. `getRatingSummary()`<br>6. `getReviews()`<br>7. `recordLateCancellation()`<br>&emsp;- on `RunCancelled`<br>8. `getOrganizerReliability()`<br>9. `logRunRecord()`<br>10. `getRunHistory()`<br>11. `setRunHistoryVisibility()` | 1. **Event Broker (Kafka)**<br>&emsp;> publish `AttendanceOpened` (Notification)<br>&emsp;> publish `RatingUpdated` (Buddy Discovery)<br>&emsp;> consume `RunEnded` (Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> consume `RunCancelled` (Run Scheduling) |
| **Trust & Safety Service**<br>Supporting subdomain<br>*Data: Trust & Safety DB (PostgreSQL)* | 1. `grantVerifiedBadge()`<br>&emsp;- on `PhoneVerified`<br>2. `getVerificationStatus()`<br>3. `reportRunner()`<br>4. `reportReview()`<br>5. `getModerationQueue()`<br>6. `reviewVerification()`<br>7. `suspendAccount()` | 1. **Identity Service** (gRPC)<br>&emsp;> `lockAccount()`<br>2. **Event Broker (Kafka)**<br>&emsp;> publish `RunnerVerified` (Buddy Discovery, Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> publish `VerificationRejected` (Buddy Discovery, Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> publish `AccountSuspended` (Buddy Discovery, Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> consume `PhoneVerified` (Identity) |
| **Identity Service**<br>Generic subdomain<br>*Data: Identity DB (PostgreSQL)* | 1. `register()`<br>2. `login()`<br>3. `logout()`<br>4. `refreshToken()`<br>5. `sendPhoneOtp()`<br>6. `verifyPhoneOtp()`<br>7. `verifyEmailOtp()`<br>8. `requestPasswordReset()`<br>9. `resetPassword()`<br>10. `lockAccount()`<br>&emsp;- gRPC | 1. **SMS/OTP Provider** (SMS/OTP Adapter)<br>&emsp;> `sendSms()`<br>2. **Email Provider** (Email Adapter)<br>&emsp;> `sendEmail()`<br>3. **Event Broker (Kafka)**<br>&emsp;> publish `PhoneVerified` (Trust & Safety)<br>&emsp;> publish `ContactChanged` (Notification) |
| **Notification Service**<br>Generic subdomain<br>*Data: Notification DB (PostgreSQL)* | 1. `sendNotification()`<br>&emsp;- on `BuddyRequestSent`, `BuddyRequestAnswered`, `RunScheduled`, `RunChanged`, `RunCancelled`, `ReminderDue`, `ForecastChanged`, `AttendanceOpened`<br>2. `updateContact()`<br>&emsp;- on `ContactChanged`<br>3. `getNotifications()`<br>4. `updatePreferences()` | 1. **Email Provider** (Email Adapter)<br>&emsp;> `sendEmail()`<br>2. **Event Broker (Kafka)**<br>&emsp;> consume `ContactChanged` (Identity)<br>&emsp;> consume `BuddyRequestSent` (Buddy Discovery)<br>&emsp;> consume `BuddyRequestAnswered` (Buddy Discovery)<br>&emsp;> consume `RunScheduled` (Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> consume `RunChanged` (Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> consume `RunCancelled` (Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> consume `ReminderDue` (Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> consume `ForecastChanged` (Run Scheduling)<br>&emsp;> consume `AttendanceOpened` (Reputation) |
